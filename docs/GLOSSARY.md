# Glossary

## AED
Adaptive Experience Director.

## Plus Card Obstacle
A specific obstacle placed in a level map that can affect card/deck planning.

## Extra Card
A card added by a defined gameplay/director rule.

## Wild Card
A special card/reward that may alter the playable deck or director plan.

## Rescue
A recovery mechanism intended to help the player from an undesirable gameplay state.

## Verified
Directly supported by source/specification.

## Proposed
New behavior awaiting approval.
